About Us

A family-owned business bringing smiles, magic, and quality character entertainment to the Triangle.

Hi, I'm Krystal!

I'm the owner of Party like a Princess LLC. Inspired by my beautiful children, I started this company to bring smiles, magic, and most importantly, quality character entertainment to the Triangle.

It is an absolute honor to serve the families of the Raleigh area, and I promise to always help create one-of-a-kind and everlasting memories. Every party we host — whether at your home or at our studio — is crafted with the same love and attention to detail that I'd want for my own children.

Our characters are unique and inspired by timeless tales, ensuring that each experience is truly special. We are not affiliated with any licensed characters, as our focus is on creating original and extraordinary adventures.

With love & sparkle, Krystal ✨
